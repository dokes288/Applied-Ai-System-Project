# Artist Notes

A small custom knowledge document used as a **second retrieval source** by the
RAG pipeline (alongside `data/songs.csv`). Each line gives per-artist context the
structured CSV does not capture. Format: `- **Artist** — note`.

- **Neon Echo** — A synth-pop duo with glossy, 1980s-inspired production, equally at home on bright dance-floor cuts and moody late-night electronic tracks.
- **LoRoom** — A lo-fi producer focused on mellow, study-friendly beats and warm, unhurried textures.
- **Voltline** — A hard-driving rock act built on fast tempos, distortion, and aggressive energy.
- **Paper Lanterns** — An ambient-leaning lo-fi project known for soft, rainy-day soundscapes and gentle acoustic warmth.
- **Max Pulse** — A high-intensity pop artist making punchy, electronic, gym-ready anthems.
- **Orbit Bloom** — An ambient composer crafting spacious, dreamy, nearly beatless atmospheres.
- **Slow Stereo** — A jazz outfit with warm, nostalgic, coffee-house instrumentation.
- **Indigo Parade** — An indie-pop band with bright, upbeat, festival-friendly hooks.
